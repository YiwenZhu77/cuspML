# Historical reproduction

These files preserve the earlier random-split analysis and its recorded metrics.
They do not reproduce the revised temporal manuscript. The historical Newell
baseline also used the wrong function: a 2007 coupling proxy raised to 2/3.
Use `../reproduce.py` and `../current/` for the corrected 2006 baseline and current results.

Old input files and random-trained weights at Zenodo belong to this historical
analysis. They must not replace `current/data` when reproducing the revised paper.
Historical commands may require their original paths and environment.
